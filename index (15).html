Our Story

It started with one-on-one coaching, and a friendship across two countries.

DatechX Solutions began in 2025 through a shared vision between Oluwafunso Damilola Soyoye and his longtime friend Abosede Yomi, based in the United Kingdom. Together they set out to bridge technology, business and practical learning — not another training brand, but a company that could solve real business problems while helping people build real careers in data.

Each founder focused on their strength: Abosede led marketing, brand awareness and advertising strategy, while Oluwafunso focused on business operations, curriculum design, learning materials, mentoring and consulting engagements. The company started modestly, with one-on-one online sessions — and every class strengthened the belief that practical learning creates lasting impact.

By the time DatechX Solutions officially launched in 2026, more than 20 professionals had already been trained through personalized online learning — a foundation for a much larger mission across Africa.
